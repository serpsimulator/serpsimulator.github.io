How to Use the LinkedIn Post Preview Tool

Start by entering your full name and professional headline — these appear directly below your avatar in the feed card, exactly as LinkedIn renders them. The live mockup updates with every keystroke, and #hashtags and @mentions are automatically highlighted in LinkedIn blue. Tips for Writing High-Performing LinkedIn Posts

1

Win the "See More" Click with a Strong First Line

LinkedIn shows only about 210 characters before truncating. Your opening sentence must be compelling enough to make connections click "more." Use the preview to verify your hook lands above the cut-off.

2

Use Short Paragraphs & Line Breaks

Dense walls of text kill engagement on LinkedIn. Write one or two sentences per paragraph, hit Enter between ideas, and check the preview to ensure your post is scannable and easy to read in the feed.

3

Stick to 3–5 Focused Hashtags

LinkedIn's algorithm responds well to targeted hashtags. Using 3–5 relevant tags — not 20 — signals topical authority. Verify your hashtags render correctly in the preview before posting.

4

Include an Image or Document for More Reach

Visual posts routinely outperform text-only updates on LinkedIn. Upload your image or select the Document type to preview the PDF card and confirm cropping and layout before publishing.

5

Stay Within 3,000 Characters for Organic Reach

LinkedIn allows up to 3,000 characters per post. The counter in this tool tracks your character usage in real time with a progress bar so you never accidentally over-write or waste space.

6

End with a Genuine Question or CTA

Posts that ask a direct question at the end generate significantly more comments. Confirm in the preview that your call-to-action is visible at the bottom of the expanded post without being buried.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this LinkedIn post preview tool free? ▼
Yes — completely free with no account required, no watermarks and no usage limits. Open the page, compose your post, and preview it live without signing up for anything.
Does this tool post to LinkedIn for me? ▼
No. This is a simulation and preview tool only. It does not connect to the LinkedIn API. Once your post looks perfect here, copy the text and post it yourself through LinkedIn.com or the LinkedIn mobile app.
Where does LinkedIn cut off the "See More" link? ▼
LinkedIn typically truncates post text at around 210 characters on desktop and mobile feeds, hiding the rest behind a "…more" button. This tool shows you exactly where that cut-off falls in real time so you can craft a hook that grabs attention before it.
What is the LinkedIn post character limit? ▼
LinkedIn allows up to 3,000 characters per post. The progress bar in this tool tracks your usage continuously. While the full 3,000 characters are allowed, most high-performing posts fall between 600 and 1,500 characters.
What image size is recommended for LinkedIn posts? ▼
LinkedIn recommends 1200 × 627 px (1.91:1 ratio) for shared images, or 1080 × 1080 px for square posts. Upload either to the previewer to see how the image fills the post card at desktop resolution before publishing.
Is my content or image stored anywhere? ▼
No. Everything runs entirely inside your browser using the FileReader API and JavaScript. No text, image or personal data is ever sent to any server — your content stays completely private on your own device.
