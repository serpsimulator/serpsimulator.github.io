Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 1, 2026

At SERP Preview, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how we keep it safe when you use our SERP simulator and social media preview tools.

1. Information We Collect

Usage Data: We may collect non-personally identifiable information such as your browser type, language preference, referring site, and the date and time of each visitor request. This helps us understand how visitors use our website.

Input Data: Any text or URLs you enter into our preview tools (titles, descriptions, etc.) are processed locally in your browser or used temporarily to generate the preview. We do not store this data on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used solely to:

  • Provide and maintain our preview services.
  • Improve the user experience and interface.
  • Analyze website traffic and trends via Google Analytics.

3. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience. These are small files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and understand site traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not function properly as a result.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics to monitor and analyze web traffic. Google Analytics may collect data such as your IP address. For more information on how Google uses data, please visit "How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps".

5. Data Security

While we do not collect sensitive personal data, we implement industry-standard security measures to protect the integrity of our website and any non-personal data we do collect.
