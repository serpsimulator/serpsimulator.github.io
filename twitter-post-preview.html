How to Use the Twitter / X Post Preview Tool

Getting started takes under a minute. Enter your display name and @handle in the fields provided, then type or paste your tweet into the content box — the live mockup on the right updates with every keystroke. Tips for Writing High-Performing Tweets & X Posts

1

Front-load Your Most Valuable Idea

Twitter/X users make a split-second decision to read or scroll. Use the preview to confirm your strongest sentence or hook is visible in the first line — before any line break or image.

2

Respect the 280-Character Limit

Watch the ring counter in real time. Tweets that use 70–120 characters tend to earn higher engagement because they're punchy and easy to retweet without editing. Check it here first.

3

Use 1–2 Relevant Hashtags (Not More)

Research shows tweets with one or two hashtags get roughly double the engagement of those with none — but engagement drops sharply above two. Verify hashtag highlighting in the preview.

4

Images Drive More Clicks

Tweets with images receive up to 3× more engagement than text-only posts. Upload your visual to the previewer and confirm the crop looks right before posting to the live timeline.

5

Use Threads for Long-form Thinking

When a single tweet isn't enough, threads dramatically outperform standalone posts for reach. Enable Thread Mode in this tool to see exactly how your first and second tweets connect visually.

6

Preview in Dark Mode Before Posting

Over 80% of Twitter/X users enable dark mode on mobile. Toggle the dark preview here to ensure your image, text and any overlaid copy remain legible against the dark timeline background.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Twitter / X post preview tool free? ▼
Yes — completely free with no account required, no watermarks and no usage limits. Open the tool, compose your tweet, and preview it instantly.
Does this tool post to Twitter or X for me? ▼
No. This is a simulation and mockup tool only. It does not connect to the Twitter or X API. You preview your content here, then copy it and post it yourself through Twitter.com or the X app.
What is the tweet character limit on X? ▼
Standard Twitter/X accounts have a 280-character limit per tweet. X Premium (formerly Twitter Blue) subscribers can post longer-form content. This tool's ring counter tracks the standard 280-character limit in real time and alerts you when you're close to or over the cap.
What image size should I use for a Twitter / X post? ▼
X recommends 1200 × 675 px for a 16:9 landscape image. For a square image use 1080 × 1080 px. The previewer will show you exactly how either size renders inside the tweet card's rounded container at desktop resolution.
How do hashtags and @mentions get highlighted? ▼
The tool automatically scans your tweet text for words beginning with # or @ and applies the X-blue colour to them in real time — no extra steps needed. This mirrors how the real timeline renders tags and mentions.
Is my tweet text or image stored anywhere? ▼
No. Everything runs entirely in your browser. Images are processed locally with the FileReader API and the canvas element. No text or image data is ever sent to any server, so your content remains completely private.
